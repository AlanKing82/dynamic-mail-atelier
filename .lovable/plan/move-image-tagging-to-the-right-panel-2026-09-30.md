# Move image tagging to the right panel

## Changes
- Remove the tag editor from above the upload area, leaving the left side focused on upload, search, filters, and browsing.
- Place the same single tag editor in the right-hand panel above the image actions.
- When no image is selected, keep the editor available for choosing tags before the next upload, with clear contextual wording.
- When an image is selected or newly uploaded, use that same editor to update its tags immediately.
- Keep custom tag entry, Enter/comma submission, removable tag chips, and quick tags: logo, banner, product, and footer.
- Preserve automatic selection of newly uploaded images and clearing of filters so the new image remains visible.
